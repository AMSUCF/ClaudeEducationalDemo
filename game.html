Principal's Dilemma

A Decision-Based Game About AI in Education

You are the principal of a public high school. Over the next three years, you'll face mounting pressure to adopt AI systems in your school.

Your decisions will affect:

  • Student Equity: Are all students receiving fair opportunities?
  • Budget Health: Can you keep the school financially stable?
  • Test Scores: Are students meeting standardized benchmarks?
  • Teacher Morale: Are your teachers supported and empowered?
  • Curriculum Autonomy: Do you maintain control over what and how you teach?

There are no perfect choices. Every decision involves trade-offs, and external pressures will constrain your options. Your goal is to navigate these competing demands as thoughtfully as possible.

The game has multiple endings based on your choices and final metrics. Can you maintain your educational values under pressure?
